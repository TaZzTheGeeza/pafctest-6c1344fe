# Parent Practical Homework Showcase

## Goal
Create a short 16:9 video for parents showing how practical football homework is being introduced gradually and how families complete it through the parent login.

## Story
- Introduce practical homework as a simple way for children to keep learning between sessions.
- Open the real Homework area through a parent account.
- Show the instructions and an embedded coaching video.
- Cut to realistic football training footage of a child completing the activity.
- Return to the real page to upload photo/video proof and answer the question sheet.
- Show coach feedback, likes, and Homework Star of the Week.
- Close by reassuring parents that everything stays together in the PAFC Hub.

## Creative direction
- **Style:** Warm parent explainer with a polished PAFC broadcast finish.
- **Palette:** Deep black, PAFC gold, and off-white.
- **Typography:** Oswald-style headings with clear Inter body text.
- **Motion:** Gentle screenshot pans, clean gold wipes, and purposeful cuts between the website and practical training.
- **Voice:** Clear British female narration, recorded separately for every scene so it always matches the picture.

## Technical details
- Capture real parent-facing Homework screens from the running website.
- Generate realistic football practice clips and mix them with the screenshots.
- Produce approximately 55–70 seconds at 1920×1080, 30fps.
- Export an H.264 MP4 and verify picture, narration, duration, and file size.
